# Field-tech copilot

> TL;DR — A voice-first assistant for technicians. Hands-free in the truck; no typing on a phone in the rain.

## The pain point

Field techs lose time on data entry: filling in job notes, looking up part numbers, scheduling follow-ups. Most fail to log half of what they did. Sizing data lands at iter-3.

## What we build

1. **Voice capture** — speak the job log, the model structures it.
2. **Manual / parts lookup** — voice query against your equipment library.
3. **Callback scheduling** — "schedule the return visit for Tuesday afternoon" → calendar entry in the FSM.

## What you keep

- The copilot, your equipment library, your job-note structure.
- Offline mode for when service drops in a basement.
- Per-tech usage metrics.
