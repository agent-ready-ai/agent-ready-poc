# How we engage

## Shape of the work

Every engagement is fixed-scope, fixed-fee, with a production handoff at the end. No "pilot programs" that drift for six months. If we can't ship to production in 4–8 weeks, the scope is wrong — we either tighten or step away.

## The four-phase arc

1. **Diagnose (week 1)** — we shadow the workflow. No slide decks, no questionnaires — we ride the truck, sit on the phones, watch the estimator.
2. **Scope (week 2)** — written 1-pager: what we're building, what it costs, what "done" looks like, what could go wrong.
3. **Build (weeks 3–6)** — we ship to a staging environment your team can poke at, then to production.
4. **Handoff (weeks 7–8)** — runbooks, evals, documentation, training. You can run it without us.

## What to expect

- Weekly check-ins, no exception.
- A single channel (your choice — Slack, email, SMS) where decisions get made and recorded.
- A "kill date" baked into every engagement — if we miss the production date, you don't pay the final invoice.
